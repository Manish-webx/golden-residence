About Indiabulls High Rise

Refined High-Rise Luxury Redefined

Indiabulls Sector 104 Gurgaon is the latest new launch residential project situated on Dwarka Expressway, one of the most promising growth corridors in NCR. Spread over 6 acres, this premium project has 4 iconic towers rising G+38 floors high, offering a stunning skyline to Gurgaon. Out of the 4 towers, 2 have already been sold to Airport Authority of India, so only limited inventory is left for homebuyers and investors, making this a rare and exclusive opportunity. The project offers thoughtfully designed residences with spacious layouts, 2BHK + Servant units of 1500 sq.ft and 3BHK + Servant units of 2100 sq.ft. Each home is crafted with attention to detail and comes with vitrified tile flooring, VRV AC units, premium fittings and 5 lifts per tower including 4 passenger and 1 service lift for smooth movement. One dedicated car parking is included in the price and 2 level basement parking for convenience, so residents can live life seamlessly that blends luxury with practicality.

One of the biggest USP of Indiabulls Sector 104 is the grand 50,000 sq.ft clubhouse which offers unmatched lifestyle and recreational facilities. Residents can indulge in multiple indoor and outdoor sports activities including 2 cricket practice nets, 2 lawn tennis courts, 1 basketball court, 2 squash courts and 2 badminton courts. The beautifully designed swimming pool and stepped green landscaping adds to the charm of this modern residential community, creating a perfect balance between active living and relaxation. Every nook and corner of the project has been designed to enhance lifestyle, where families can bond, children can play and individuals can pursue their fitness and wellness goals.

FAQ

Questions Answered

1. What is Indiabulls High Rise, Sector 104?

Indiabulls High Rise is an iconic, ultra-luxury residential high-rise development in Sector 104, Gurugram along the Dwarka Expressway. It offers world-class fully furnished 3 BHK and 3 BHK + study residences designed for refined urban living.

2. What is the starting price for residences at Indiabulls High Rise?

Prices start at ₹2.6 Cr onwards for the Fully Furnished Luxury 3 BHK residences (approx. 2150 sq.ft.), and ₹3.1 Cr onwards for the 3 BHK + Study Grand residences (approx. 2650 sq.ft.).

3. What does "Fully Furnished Luxury" include?

Each residence at Indiabulls High Rise is meticulously curated and move-in ready. It includes bespoke designer living and dining furniture, Italian marble flooring, state-of-the-art modular kitchen with branded appliances, designer built-in wardrobes, VRV air conditioning, and premium bathroom fittings.

4. Where is Indiabulls High Rise located?

The project is strategically located in Sector 104, Gurugram, directly accessible from the Dwarka Expressway (NH-248BB). This prime corridor offers seamless 15-minute connectivity to IGI Airport & Aerocity, 10 minutes to Yashobhoomi Convention Centre, and 20 minutes to DLF Cyber City.

5. Is the project RERA approved?

Yes, Indiabulls High Rise is a fully RERA Approved Project. We adhere strictly to RERA compliance, ensuring 100% legal transparency, institutional escrow fund protection, and committed delivery schedules.

6. What are the key clubhouse and lifestyle amenities?

Residents enjoy exclusive access to Indiabulls Sky Club & Spa, featuring a temperature-controlled infinity pool, rooftop sky deck and lounge, high-end fitness studio, private mini-theatre, indoor games arena, and landscaped podium sky gardens.

7. What floor plan configurations are available?

We offer two distinguished layouts: 3 BHK Fully Furnished Luxury Residences (approx. 2150 sq.ft., starting at ₹2.6 Cr) and 3 BHK + Study Grand Luxury Residences (approx. 2650 sq.ft., starting at ₹3.1 Cr), designed for families seeking generous space and elegance.

8. What are the payment plans and home loan options?

We offer attractive, construction-linked and flexible payment schedules tailored to buyer requirements. Home loan facilities are approved by all major financial institutions including SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Axis Bank.

9. How can I schedule a VIP site visit or private preview?

You can schedule a complimentary VIP private site visit and show apartment preview by calling or messaging our luxury sales desk on WhatsApp at +91 93-5555-8585 or submitting an inquiry through this website. Private visits are hosted 7 days a week from 9:00 AM to 6:30 PM.
